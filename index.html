Auction Sheet Verification You Can Trust

Before you commit to a bid, it pays to verify real auction sheet details rather than rely on a translated summary from a seller. JDM Auction Bangladesh works directly with Japanese auction houses to confirm that grading, chassis numbers, and condition notes on the sheet match the vehicle you are actually bidding on.

A real auction sheet verified against the original auction house record protects you from mismatched chassis numbers, inflated grading, or hidden accident history that a reprint or edited copy can easily disguise.

Whether you are bidding for the first time or sourcing multiple units for resale, our team checks the sheet against the source before you place a bid, so your decision is based on facts rather than a seller's word.

Why Auction Sheet Verification Matters

Fake or altered auction sheets are one of the most common risks buyers face when importing Japanese vehicles. A verified sheet confirms the grade, mileage, and condition remarks actually came from the auction house and were not changed after the fact.
